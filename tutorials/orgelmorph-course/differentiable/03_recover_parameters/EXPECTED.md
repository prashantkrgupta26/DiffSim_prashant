# D3 — expected results (self-check)

Running `python run.py` (defaults: 9×9 nodes, 6 steps, BDF1, Flory–Huggins
$A=1$; truth $\chi=2.5,\ \kappa=0.01$; guess $\chi=2.1,\ \kappa=0.02$;
bounds keep the rollout interior) reproduces the recovery below.

**Gradient self-check** (adjoint vs finite differences, off-truth point):

| gradient | adjoint | finite diff | rel |
|---|---|---|---|
| $dL/d\chi$   | $-1.734527\mathrm{e}{-1}$ | $-1.734527\mathrm{e}{-1}$ | $5.7\mathrm{e}{-11}$ |
| $dL/d\kappa$ | $+1.723537\mathrm{e}{+0}$ | $+1.723537\mathrm{e}{+0}$ | $1.2\mathrm{e}{-10}$ |

**Recovery:**

| param | truth | guess | recovered | \|err\| |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\chi$    | 2.50000 | 2.10000 | 2.500000 | $2.2\mathrm{e}{-11}$ |
| $\kappa$  | 0.01000 | 0.02000 | 0.010000 | $1.8\mathrm{e}{-11}$ |

- loss $6.17\mathrm{e}{-2}$ (guess) $\to 1.6\mathrm{e}{-20}$ (recovered), in
  13 optimiser evaluations.

**What must be true regardless of hardware:**

- **The fitting-loss gradient matches finite differences** (`rel < 1e-6`;
  measured $\sim10^{-10}$). The optimiser is only as good as its gradient;
  this is the self-check it rests on.
- **The loss collapses** by many orders of magnitude — the target was
  generated by the forward model itself, so a perfect fit exists and
  gradient descent finds it.
- **Both parameters reach truth** (to $\sim10^{-11}$). $\chi$ and $\kappa$
  leave distinguishable fingerprints on the morphology (interaction sets
  *how much* it demixes; the gradient penalty sets the *interface width*),
  so the inverse problem is well-posed at this rollout length.

Exact iteration counts and floor values depend on the BLAS/optimiser
build; the qualitative result (loss collapses, parameters $\to$ truth)
must hold. If the loss stalls far above zero, the two parameters are not
separately identifiable in your setup — shorten or lengthen the rollout
and re-examine.
