\chapter{The pressure-projection engine}
\label{ch:c2}

\emph{Module: \file{02\_pressure\_projection/}. The scalable engine.
Predictor $\to$ SPD pressure-Poisson $\to$ velocity correction; the same
lid-driven cavity, the same answer; and why this is the 100M-DOF path.}

\section{The Helmholtz--Leray split}
The projection engine implements Algorithm~1 of the Helmholtz--Leray VMS
draft (\file{leray.py:1--23}). Per step, with $p^\*$ the extrapolated
pressure (order~1: $p^\*=\hat p^n$):
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Momentum predictor} (nonlinear, Picard): solve the monolithic
  block with the pressure DOFs pinned to $p^\*$'s nodal values, so the
  momentum rows see $\grad p^\*$ through the coupling
  (\file{leray.py:6--13}, \file{719--830}).
\item \textbf{PPE} --- the SPD pressure-Poisson for the increment
  $\phi=\hat p - p^\*$:
  \[
  \inner{\grad\phi}{\grad q} = \sigma\,\inner{\hat u - \tauM r_m(\hat u,p^\*)}{\grad q}
  \]
  with the h-based $\tauM$ (\file{leray.py:14--19}, \file{948--1106}).
\item \textbf{Velocity correction} (fine scale retained):
  $u^{n+1}=\hat u - \tfrac1\sigma(\grad\hat p - \grad p^\*)$.
\item $p^\*\leftarrow\hat p$ (first-order incremental).
\end{enumerate}

\begin{keybox}\textbf{Key idea --- why projection scales.} The pressure
step is a \textbf{symmetric positive-definite} Poisson problem. Unlike the
indefinite saddle, it is the natural target for algebraic multigrid $+$ CG
--- the mandated path to the 100M-DOF hero (\file{leray\_sbm.py:19--23}).
This is the whole reason projection exists alongside the monolithic
oracle.\end{keybox}

\section{The VMS-consistent PPE (\texttt{consistent\_projection})}
Na\"ively splitting the projection \emph{breaks faithfulness}: the split's
steady state is generally \emph{not} the monolithic steady state. The
\code{consistent_projection} mode (the 2026-07-23 fix,
\file{leray.py:48--72}) is the single switch that turns on the coherent
VMS-stabilized Helmholtz--Leray set so the monolithic steady state becomes
a \textbf{fixed point} of the split. It bundles six items:
\begin{description}
\item[\#1 PSPG-consistent PPE operator] the coarse divergence is kept
  \emph{collocated} with the test $q$ (NOT integrated by parts), so the
  split's discrete incompressibility matches the monolithic PSPG continuity
  row term-for-term (\file{leray.py:1007--1034}). The full-flux by-parts
  path fabricates a spurious boundary term $\sigma(\uh\cdot n,q)_\Gamma$ at
  an open outflow.
\item[\#2 fine scale $u'=-\tauM r_m$ in BOTH the PPE source AND the L2
  correction] with the consistent mass $D=G^\top$ (\file{leray.py:53--61}).
\item[\#3 disjoint outflow BCs (Baskar's rule)] velocity is left free at the
  outflow (natural do-nothing $\partial_n u=0$), while the pressure
  \emph{correction} $p'=0$ is imposed as a Dirichlet condition on the
  outflow nodes. Authoritative: $\grad u\cdot n=0$ in the predictor
  \emph{only}, $p'=0$ in the PPE \emph{only} (\file{leray.py:62--69}).
\item[\#4 rotational-incremental pressure update (Timmermans 1996)]
  $p=p^\*+\phi-\nu\,\grad\!\cdot\hat u$, valid for constant $\nu$
  (\file{leray.py:1114--1168}).
\item[\#5/\#6] a $P_1$ boundary-vorticity outflow term and backflow
  (directional do-nothing) stabilization, both on the outflow.
\end{description}
Two secular-drift cures ride with the mode: the rotational correction $q$
is pinned to zero on the outflow rows and, for immersed walls, on the
SBM/wall nodes --- the latter is \textbf{the drag fix} for weak-Nitsche
bodies (Ch.~\ref{ch:c3}).

\section{Fine scale threaded through all three sub-solves}
The fine-scale velocity $-\tauM r_m$ is computed once in the PPE pass and
\emph{stashed} (\code{fs_vel}, \file{leray.py:973}) so the same $u'$ enters
the PPE source \emph{and} the velocity correction --- the consistency that
makes the correction a true discrete projection.

\section{The composed SBM engine}
\code{LeraySBMStepper} (\file{leray\_sbm.py:81--160}) \emph{composes} the
base projection stepper with an immersed-geometry oracle (it does not fork
it). It runs the surrogate pipeline once, builds the shifted-Nitsche face
block once (\code{sbm_vector_dirichlet}), and injects it into the predictor
via the \code{extra_block}/\code{sbm_nodes} hook. The box inflow/walls are
strong; the immersed body is weak (SBM). This is the engine of
Ch.~\ref{ch:c4}--\ref{ch:c5}.

\section{Verification: the same cavity, the same answer}
The module re-runs the Chapter~\ref{ch:c1} lid-driven cavity through the
projection engine on the same level-4 mesh. The projection tracks the
monolithic to within the coarse-mesh band:
\[
\max|\text{proj}-\text{mono}| = \LdcProjMono,\qquad
\max|\text{proj}-\text{Ghia}| = \LdcProjGhia.
\]
The pointwise projection $\|\grad\!\cdot u\| = \LdcDivProj$ is finite and
bounded (not zero) --- the same weak-divergence story as
Ch.~\ref{ch:c1}. The right divergence gate is the \textbf{PPE-space
solenoidality identity} $\|\sigma B^\top\hat u - K_p\phi\|\approx0$, which
is machine-zero at the fixed point.
