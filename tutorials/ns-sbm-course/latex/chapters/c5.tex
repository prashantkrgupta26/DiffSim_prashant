\chapter{Three dimensions --- the SBM sphere}
\label{ch:c5}

\emph{Module: \file{05\_three\_dimensions/}. The end-to-end SBM-NS engine in
3-D; the shift $d\neq0$ is genuinely active; the cuDSS/AMGX solver path; an
honest verdict on the two engines.}

\section{Geometry}
An immersed \code{Sphere(center=(0.35,0.5,0.5), R=0.12)} carved from a
unit-box octree channel ($\lambda=0.5$), $\texttt{ndof}=4$, strong inflow
($x=0$) $+$ lateral walls ($y,z$), free outflow ($x=1$), weak no-slip on the
sphere. The sphere is not grid-aligned, so the SBM shift $d\neq0$ is
genuinely active in 3-D. At level~4: $n_{\text{free}}=\SpNfree$,
\SpFaces{} surrogate faces, $D/h=\SpDoverh$, $d_{\max}=\SpDmax$.

\section{The honest 3-D verdict}
The two engines part ways in 3-D, and the course is explicit about it
(documented in \file{tests/baselines/p2r0\_task10\_sphere.json}):
\begin{description}
\item[MONOLITHIC 3-D SBM-NS --- the working drag path.] Stable and physical:
  it recovers from the startup transient to a positive steady drag,
  \[
  \Cd = +\SpCdMono\quad(\Rey=100,\ \alpha=10,\ \text{level }4),
  \]
  reproducing the M1b sphere lock. This is the hero result for 3-D drag. It
  scales via GPU direct (\code{solver="cudss"}) or block-preconditioned
  FGMRES past the host-splu wall (${\sim}$level 5 / 143k DOF).
\item[PROJECTION $+$ volumetric SBM (\code{LeraySBMStepper}) --- de-risked
  but not yet a drag path.] The composition is de-risked (finite,
  axisymmetric $|C_{\text{lat}}|\ll|\Cd|$, BDF2 engages, PPE-space weak
  divergence machine-zero), \emph{but} its long-time drag transient is
  unstable at feasible 3-D mesh (the coupled iteration's $\Cd$ diverges
  monotonically while staying finite --- a documented R2 item). So in 3-D we
  validate the pipeline invariants and take drag from the monolithic.
\end{description}

\begin{keybox}\textbf{Key idea.} The confined unit-box domain inflates
$\Cd$ above the unconfined literature value; \emph{both} engines see the
same confinement, so the bar is faithfulness (and, in 3-D, the
monolithic's physical steady state), not the literature number.\end{keybox}

\section{Solver path}
Host \code{splu} is used for the small level-4 case here (${\sim}$seconds
per step). To scale up, build the fixture on a CUDA device and route the
monolithic solve through \code{solver="cudss"} (GPU direct) or
\code{solver="blockamgx"} (block-preconditioned FGMRES: a Cahouet--Chabard
Schur complement $+$ AMG-on-$F$), both wired in the sphere fixture's
\code{monolithic_cd} (\file{p2r0\_task10\_sphere\_derisk.py:179--281}). The
\emph{projection} engine's SPD PPE is the AMG-friendly one; that is the
subject of Ch.~\ref{ch:c6}.
