## 1. Validation-target finding (flagged for Baskar)

**2512.16390 is a BINARY PCE11:PCBM blend**, not a ternary.  The P1
memo staged S2 as "ternary annealing" on the assumption the paper's
system was ternary.  What actually needs the ternary machinery is the
paper's Fig-6 case: TWO crystallinity fields (PCBM crystallization +
inert PCE11 crystallites) on a binary composition — in our
solvent-eliminated layout (psi only on retained species, K <= M) that
requires M = 2 with a small inert trace third species (2 v%; recorded
deviation with ANALYTIC consequence: the trace-exchange SD mode is
2.6x slower / 1.5x coarser than the true binary — dispersion computed
2026-07-10).  The Fig-4 case (only PCBM crystallizes; PCE11
crystallization omitted BY THE PAPER) maps exactly onto (M=1, K=1).

## 2. Parameter provenance

The arXiv PDF has no SI.  The COMPLETE production parameter set was
recovered from the authors' Zenodo dataset (10.5281/zenodo.17633571):
MATLAB run decks `Article_Simulations/*_run*.m` per figure, plus their
own post-processed kinetics curves (MATLAB .fig = MAT files, parsed
with scipy.io) which provide QUANTITATIVE replication targets nowhere
stated in the main text.  Extraction table in the test module
docstring.  Notable deck facts:

- Their 2-D mesh is ny = 1 with dy = 1 nm: the S1c "2-D noise depth"
  assumption is their exact convention — FDT nucleation onsets are
  calibration-MATCHED here (sigma_AC = 1), unlike the 2310.11844 case.
- Their theta is heuristic marker actualization
  (Flag.Cristal.Oriented = 2), confirming the P1 Sec 8 verdict.
- Their run deck carries a numerical simplex barrier
  (PHIbarnum = 5e-2, kbarnum = 1e-5): the analytic PCBM-rich binodal
  at chi = 1.2649, N = (132.7, 1) is ~0.9995 (essentially pure), but
  their own measured liquid plateau is 0.968 — i.e. THEIR equilibria
  include the barrier.  Our house b_reg = 3e-4 (u0 units) reproduces
  both of their liquid plateaus (0.972 / 0.217 vs their 0.968 /
  0.21-0.243) and is used in all S2c runs (calibrated stand-in for
  their unpublished barrier form).
- Their production dt was CAPPED at 0.0304 s -> ~9000 implicit steps
  of a 4M-dof system per run (plus 20 runs per case): cluster-scale
  budgets.  Our runs scale the domain down (recorded per case) per the
  20-min budget rule.

## 3. Solver findings (S2 build)

- The multicomponent fast/slow Onsager matrices (anchor Eqs. 11-12)
  with Vignes self-diffusion NEED the exact dLam/dphi, dLam/dpsi
  Jacobian blocks: frozen-mobility Picard (the wodo var_mob lesson)
  does NOT transfer to 3-decade composition-dependent mobilities
  (measured: 13-43-iteration Newton tails, ladder collapse to
  dt ~ 1e-4 s).
- Deep-quench SD purification additionally needs BOTH the ||r||_2
  backtracking line search (new `line_search=True`) AND the simplex
  barrier; with them the march rides dt = 0.02-0.5 s through SD at
  3-6 iterations/step at the paper's exact kappa.
- cuDSS vs splu on the 2M+2K monolithic Jacobian at 256^2:
  refactorize+solve 11 ms vs 2.3 s (COLAMD fill 13M nnz) — cuDSS is
  the production solver for S2 (the wodo DirectSolver pattern).
