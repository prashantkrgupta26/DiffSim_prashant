\chapter*{How to use this packet}
Every chapter follows one contract: a \emph{learning outcome} (what you can
do afterwards), \emph{background} (the minimum theory), a complete,
documented, runnable script, \emph{expected results} --- measured numbers
you must reproduce, not aspirations --- and \emph{explore} questions worth
a lab-notebook entry. Each chapter also carries a \emph{performance
corner}: a measurement task in the spirit of the group's computational-cost
exercise. Run everything; distrust any claim you did not measure.

The scripts live in \texttt{tutorials/} in the repository and are the
source of truth; this packet is their typeset companion. Suggested paths:
application users A1$\to$A3$\to$A6$\to$C1$\to$D2$\to$D3; methods students
straight through; differentiable-simulation students
A1$\to$A3$\to$E1 then backfill.
